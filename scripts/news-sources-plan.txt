Ryan Report - News Sources Plan (v1, free/public-only)

Scope and constraints
- This is the first implementation pass.
- Prioritize clearly documented, freely accessible RSS/Atom feeds or public government feeds.
- Prefer official/primary sources over broad but less reliable coverage.
- Do not scrape web pages.
- Do not assume an RSS feed exists for a publisher.
- Do not use authenticated, paid, unofficial, or third-party mirror feeds.
- Exclude any source with unclear licensing or feed availability rather than guessing.
- Keep the project at $0 ongoing cost.
- No sports coverage.

Implementation principle for v1
- Reliability and source legitimacy matter more than quantity.
- The first release should use a smaller, official list rather than a broad but questionable set of feeds.
- For this first version, only verified public feed URLs will be used.
- The project should stay at $0 and avoid paid APIs or subscriptions.

Summary
The first-pass source set is intentionally conservative: Bank of Canada official feeds are the strongest foundation for a free, relevant, trustworthy Ryan Report feed layer. I am not broadening the source list until each additional feed is independently verified as a public and appropriate feed.
